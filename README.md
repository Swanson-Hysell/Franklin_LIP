# The Franklin large igneous province and Snowball Earth initiation

This repository contains the data, code, and figures associated with:

> Macdonald, F.A. and Swanson-Hysell, N.L. (2023), The Franklin large igneous province and Snowball Earth initiation, *Elements*, 19, 296–301, [doi:10.2138/gselements.19.5.296](https://doi.org/10.2138/gselements.19.5.296).

The paper synthesizes geochronologic, paleomagnetic, and paleogeographic constraints on the ca. 719 Ma Franklin LIP and evaluates its temporal and causal relationship to onset of the Sturtian Snowball Earth glaciation (ca. 717 Ma). This repository archives the underlying compilations and the notebooks that generate the data figures in the paper.

The contents are also archived at [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10152921).

## What is in the paper

The paper has three figures developed in this repository:

- **Figure 2** — map of mapped Franklin dikes, high-precision U-Pb zircon date sample locations, and paleomagnetic sites (compiled in Denyszyn et al. 2009), shown together with alternative outlines of the LIP extent (Ernst et al. 2021; this study).
- **Figure 3** — date-bar synthesis of CA-ID-TIMS U-Pb zircon dates from the Franklin LIP (Pu et al. 2022; Dufour et al. 2023) and from pre- and syn-Sturtian glacial successions in Laurentia (Macdonald et al. 2010, 2017; Cox et al. 2015), Ethiopia (MacLennan et al. 2018), Oman (Bowring et al. 2007), and South China (Lan et al. 2020). Resampling from the analytical uncertainties yields estimates for the duration of the main Franklin pulse (540 kyr; 95% range 260–860 kyr) and the time lag between the end of that pulse and Sturtian glaciation onset (~1.2 to ~1.7 Myr).
- **Figure 4** — mean Franklin paleomagnetic pole and associated paleoequator (with 95% confidence bounds) calculated from the Denyszyn et al. (2009) compilation, plotted with reconstructed Sturtian glacial deposits and other Tonian Laurentia poles.

## Repository structure

### [code/](code/)

- [Franklin_Pole_Plotting.ipynb](code/Franklin_Pole_Plotting.ipynb) — calculates the mean Franklin paleomagnetic pole (Fisher mean of filtered virtual geomagnetic poles, n>2 and α95<15°) from the Denyszyn et al. (2009) compilation and produces the pole figure in the paper (Figure 4). Uses [PmagPy](https://github.com/PmagPy/PmagPy) for pole statistics and [cartopy](https://scitools.org.uk/cartopy/) for mapping.
- [Sturtian_Franklin_Dates.ipynb](code/Sturtian_Franklin_Dates.ipynb) — imports CA-ID-TIMS U-Pb zircon datasets, computes weighted means, and produces the date-bar plot synthesizing Franklin LIP and Cryogenian glacial-succession geochronology (Figure 3). Includes the Monte Carlo resampling that yields the duration estimate for the main Franklin pulse and the lag estimates relative to glaciation onset.
- [geochron_functions.py](code/geochron_functions.py) — helper functions for weighted-mean calculation and date-bar plotting used by the geochronology notebook.
- [Franklin_Geochron.pdf](code/Franklin_Geochron.pdf) — supporting figure exported from the geochronology workflow.
- [code_output/](code/code_output/) — figure PDFs generated by the notebooks (`Franklin_pole.pdf`, `dates.pdf`, `histograms.pdf`).
- [example_conversion/](code/example_conversion/) — supporting example output.

### [data/](data/)

- [data/Geochron/](data/Geochron/) — published U-Pb zircon date tables (individual analyses with 2σ uncertainties and inclusion flags), grouped by region:
  - [Laurentia/](data/Geochron/Laurentia/) — Franklin LIP intrusions (Pu et al. 2022; Dufour et al. 2023) and pre-/syn-Sturtian volcanic and tuffaceous units (Macdonald et al. 2010; Cox et al. 2015; Macdonald et al. 2017).
  - [Ethiopia/](data/Geochron/Ethiopia/) — pre-Sturtian dates and Monte Carlo extrapolations of Sturtian onset from sedimentation rates (MacLennan et al. 2018).
  - [Oman/](data/Geochron/Oman/) — syn-glacial dates from Bowring et al. (2007).
  - [South_China/](data/Geochron/South_China/) — pre-glacial date from Lan et al. (2020).
- [data/pmag/](data/pmag/) — paleomagnetic site-mean compilation for the Franklin LIP from Denyszyn et al. (2009), including site- and pole-level rotations applied to data from Devon Island and Greenland to restore them to Laurentia coordinates. Provided as both `.csv` and as a GPlates-compatible shapefile.
- [data/gis/](data/gis/) — GIS layers used to make the map figure:
  - [Franklin_LIP/](data/gis/Franklin_LIP/) — Franklin LIP outline polygons, including the Ernst et al. (2021) compilation, an extension to the present continental margin, and a simplified intrusion-connecting outline used in this paper.
  - [Franklin_dikes/](data/gis/Franklin_dikes/) — mapped Franklin dike traces.
  - [Sturtian_Glacial/](data/gis/Sturtian_Glacial/) — locations of Sturtian glacial deposits.
  - [dates/](data/gis/dates/) — point shapefile of the Dufour et al. (2023) U-Pb sample sites.
  - [continents/](data/gis/continents/) — Torsvik & Cocks continent shapes and rotation file used for paleogeographic reconstructions.
  - [reconstructed/](data/gis/reconstructed/) — continents, dikes, dates, and pmag sites rotated to their ca. 719 Ma reconstructed positions in GPlates.
  - `Franklin_LIP.gproj`, `Franklin_mps.qgz` — GPlates and QGIS project files.

### [figures/](figures/)

Published-version figure PDFs from the paper, including the map figure (`Franklin_map_figure*.pdf`), the date synthesis (`Franklin_Sturtian_dates*.pdf`), and the pole figure (`Franklin_pole_figure.pdf`). Files marked `_updated` reflect post-publication revisions (e.g., the date-bar plot updated to incorporate the Dufour et al. 2023 dates and a Lambert projection on the map).

## Reproducing the figures

The notebooks depend on a standard scientific Python stack along with [PmagPy](https://github.com/PmagPy/PmagPy), [cartopy](https://scitools.org.uk/cartopy/), and [shapely](https://shapely.readthedocs.io/). Running the notebooks from the [code/](code/) directory regenerates the figure PDFs in [code/code_output/](code/code_output/). The map and reconstructed GIS layers are produced in QGIS and GPlates from the project files and shapefiles in [data/gis/](data/gis/).
